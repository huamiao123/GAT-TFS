# FULL FAST destination schedule ablation

Independent variable: native ascending DegreeSort versus identity destination order.
Frozen controls: original TFS v3 source, paper FAST FULL kernel, CSR, seed 12345,
H/W, 128->128->128, BF16 inputs and W, ReLU, interlayer BF16 conversion,
TR16/R64, OpenMP dynamic schedule, 32 physical cores on one socket, default
NUMA, same process and binary. Each graph checks FP32 layer 1 and BF16 layer 2
bitwise equality. Prepared two-layer E2E excludes DegreeSort and identity
initialization. Their setup times are reported separately. Per method: eight
alternating-order warmup-plus-measured repetitions. No graph-dependent policy.

The source is generated by inserting a call into the untouched original TFS
driver. The original driver still executes its paper TFS and MKL comparisons;
only ABLATION_TIME lines constitute this ablation's primary paired timing.
The two FULL methods invoke exactly the same frozen block_kernel<false>.
No trained checkpoint or classification accuracy is claimed.
