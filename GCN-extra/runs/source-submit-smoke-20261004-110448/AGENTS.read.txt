
Update all three after every experiment, including smoke tests, correctness runs, environment probes, build experiments, profiling, benchmarks, recovery runs, and formal reruns. An experiment is not complete until all three documents are synchronized.

- `ALL_EXPERIMENTS.md` must list every TFS-Train experiment chronologically, including date/time, experiment ID and run tag, purpose, dataset or fixture, model/dimensions, implementation path, partition, JobID, actual node, CPU/NUMA/thread/binding, compiler/build, input/source/binary hashes, timing boundary, repeats, correctness result, performance result, status, evidence paths, and whether it may enter a paper table. - `EXPERIMENT_ISSUES.md` must record every major issue or issue likely to recur, including discovery date, affected experiments, symptoms, root cause, diagnostic evidence, resolution/workaround, prevention rule, validation status, and related paths. - `PROJECT_PROGRESS.md` must show the current cycle, completed/in-progress/pending work, latest experiment outcomes, acceptance status, current technical decisions, deviations from plan, blockers, risks, and the exact next task. Historical progress must not be rewritten to hide failed approaches.

Before starting the next experiment, verify that the previous experiment appears in all three documents. Before reporting a milestone complete, reconcile the documents against run directories, Slurm accounting, manifests, raw logs, and hashes; chat history is not an authority.
